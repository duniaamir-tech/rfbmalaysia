About RFB Malaysia

A local partner with European technology heritage.

RFB Malaysia Sdn Bhd is part of the RFB Group. The group's heritage traces back to RFB-GOETZE GMBH, Leipzig, Germany, established in 1974. Today, RFB Malaysia focuses on technology solutions for telecom, IT, enterprise and hospitality infrastructure.

Our positioning is simple: Your Trusted Partner in Advanced Technology Solutions. We bring established European products into a local technical and procurement conversation for Malaysian and regional customers.

Why partner with RFB Malaysia?

Critical network decisions need more than a catalogue. RFB Malaysia helps teams evaluate infrastructure software, distribution hardware and specialist solutions against their operational requirements.
