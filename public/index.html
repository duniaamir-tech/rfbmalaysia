Our Heritage & Group Background

RFB Malaysia Sdn Bhd is part of the established RFB Group, tracing its engineering heritage back to 1974 through RFB-GOETZE GMBH (Leipzig, Germany). Guided globally by Group Managing Director Carsten Goetze and locally driven in Malaysia by Managing Director Amir Dunia, we combine European industrial standards with practical regional execution.

In telecommunications and critical infrastructure, shortcuts lead to network downtime. Enterprise buyers and tier-1 operators trust RFB Malaysia because we deliver verified, standards-compliant technologies backed by rigorous engineering know-how.
